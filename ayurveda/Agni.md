# Agni

Agni is the Ayurvedic and Vedic name for fire as a living principle: the flame on the altar, the heat in the kitchen, the digestive fire in the belly, and the discipline of *tapas* when heat is turned inward. This note is no longer the finished essay on that doctrine. The finished article is [In Praise of Agni](../diet/In_Praise_of_Agni.md).

The source trail now lives in two places:

- [Cooking and Agni](../source-extracts/Cooking_and_Agni.md) gathers the Ayurvedic and yogic passages on cooked food, *anna*, *rasa*, *prāṇa*, and *antaragni*.
- [Agni and Tapas](../source-extracts/Agni_and_Tapas.md) gathers the Vedic, yogic, and cross-tradition fire witnesses.

This boundary keeps the repository clean: source extracts hold the working quotation bank, this note names the doctrine, and the finished diet article carries the public argument.
