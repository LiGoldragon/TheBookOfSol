# Power, Intent, Warrior, and Freedom — Armando Torres research notes

*Armando Torres, Encuentros con el nagual: conversaciones con Carlos Castaneda* (Spanish first edition reported 2002; English edition 2004) and *La telaraña universal* (Editora Alba, 2020).

These notes distinguish catalogued wording from terms supplied as research leads. They are a bibliography-and-terminology aid, not an assertion that reported conversations or spiritual claims are historically verified.

## Terms that are anchored

The searchable preview for *La telaraña universal* lists chapters including “La Maestría del Intento,” “Decisión, Acción, Reacción,” and “Requerimientos del Intento.” Its preview makes `intento` the force connected with alignment, decisions, and committed will. The same source indexes `guerrero`, `camino del guerrero`, `poder`, `meta`, `muerte`, `silencio`, `energía`, and `videntes`.

The available preview text for *Encuentros con el nagual* gives the compact formulation: “La meta es estar consciente.” It supports a careful gloss of `meta` as an aim of awareness in this particular presentation.

| Original term | Working gloss | Reading note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `intento` | intent | Preserve the Spanish where technical precision matters. |
| `voluntad` | will | Do not flatten it into contemporary “willpower” without context. |
| `poder personal` | personal power | Technical vocabulary within a Castaneda-adjacent framework. |
| `guerrero` | warrior | A disciplined role in the text. |
| `vidente` | seer | Keep distinct from `brujo`. |
| `libertad` | freedom | The surrounding context gives it an esoteric register. |

## Four unverified “mitades”

The phrases “La mitad del brujo,” “La mitad del guerrero,” “La mitad del vidente,” and “La mitad de la libertad” have not been located as Torres titles, chapter headings, or a named four-part teaching. Keep them as leads for later page-level checking.

Castaneda-related Spanish texts do use `la otra mitad` in the tonal/nagual image, where will claims the half left outside ordinary reason. That nearby wording is insufficient to attribute the four requested phrases to Torres.
