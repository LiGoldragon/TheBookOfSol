# Dane Rudhyar — *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix* — Extracts

*A Study of the Zodiac and Planetary Activity.* Written 1942-1943; edited and republished by Michael R. Meyer in the Lost Writings of Dane Rudhyar series.

This manuscript preserves an early, more substantive formulation of Rudhyar's zodiac doctrine. Beside the temporal zodiac of *The Pulse of Life*, it describes the zodiac as a formative field, the matrix in which solar life-processes and planetary functions operate.

## The task is to say what the zodiac represents

> "There are many approaches to an interpretation of the zodiac — essentially as many as there are schools of astrology. Wherever an attempt is made to give a philosophical background to the practice of astrology, the first task is to explain what the zodiac actually represents."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*, Introduction

## The zodiac as yearly cycle of transformations

> "According to this point of view, the signs of the zodiac have no reality in themselves. They merely represent a cyclic series of basic changes in the natural experience of human beings (and of all living things on the Earth's surface), a cycle which is repeated year after year. The Sun is the essential cause of such a series of changes, and the zodiac is seen as a purely solar phenomenon."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*, Introduction

> "From this approach the zodiac can be seen as a yearly 'cycle of transformations,' the meaning of natural yearly transformations being extended to apply to the psychological-mental and social levels of human development. The zodiac, from such a point of view, is a factor of time. It is a cycle — a process."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*, Introduction

## The zodiac as surrounding matrix

> "The other basic approach to an interpretation of the nature of the zodiac considers the zodiac as a factor of space; as a static structure having an independent reality, a reality independent of the motions of the Sun, the Moon and the planets. These moving astrological entities pass through the space defined by the zodiacal signs."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*, Introduction

> "The signs have value and meaning in their own right, Sun or no Sun. They moreover 'operate' all the time, all of them — whether or not a planet is passing through them. The Earth is surrounded by the zodiac — and therefore individual and collective humanity too is living in its midst."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*, Introduction

> "In such a view, upon which this series of studies is based, the zodiac becomes the very substance of so-called astrological 'influence.' Such 'influence' is not this or that planet, not even the Sun or the Moon, but the zodiac's 'emanations.' The planets merely activate and focus or emphasize such 'emanations.' The planets and the 'lights' (Sun and Moon) produce focal points for the release of the zodiacal 'emanations' completely surrounding us. But what act upon us are the zodiacal 'emanations,' not the planets' 'rays.'"\
> — Dane Rudhyar, *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*, Introduction

## The zodiac as field and formative realm

> "The zodiac is a 'universal matrix' — thus a place; yet a matrix is not an ordinary kind of space. It is an electromagnetic 'field' upon which formative Powers are focused. It is living substance in the process of being built into an organism. It is not a 'house' or a collection of neatly defined spaces, but the crucible of life."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, quoted in Michael R. Meyer's Foreword to *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*

> "The zodiac is the formative realm of life in which the astrological Sun operates as the fountain-head of all life-processes. It is the realm of birthing, growing, maturing, decaying and dying; where substance is made and unmade; where anabolic and catabolic forces (light and shadow, integration and disintegration) operate in intense, unceasing, dynamic being."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, quoted in Michael R. Meyer's Foreword to *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*

> "We can think of a sign of the zodiac as a region in which one aspect of the solar force is concentrated in work; but if we picture this place in static terms we are greatly mistaken. The essence of the zodiac is dynamic activity at the level of the formation of substance, of polar electromagnetic energies, of life-processes."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, quoted in Michael R. Meyer's Foreword to *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*

> "The twelvefold zodiac reveals the constant workings of the formative power of Spirit in every part of each and all organic wholes; thus the correlation of the signs of the zodiac with parts of the human organism and with geodetic earth zones."\
> — Dane Rudhyar, *The Zodiac as the Universal Matrix*, Introduction
