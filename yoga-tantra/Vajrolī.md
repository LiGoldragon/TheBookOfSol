# Vajrolī: Sovereignty Made Innate

## Knowledge Before Restraint

Vajrolī (the perineal upward-draw practice that retains and reverses the vital seed) names the moment when mastery ceases to be an effort and becomes structure, when life-force is organized so thoroughly that loss no longer presents itself as a problem. What appears first as deliberate regulation matures into inward certainty, and discipline sheds its visible scaffolding.

The verse blocks below follow the *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* sequence on Vajrolī, Sahajolī, and Amarolī. The translations stay plain where the Sanskrit is concrete; doctrinal interpretation belongs in the surrounding prose.

> **svecchayā vartamāno'pi yogoktair niyamair vinā**\
> **vajrolīṃ yo vijānāti sa yogī siddhi-bhājanam**
>
> "Even living according to one's own will, without the restraints taught by yoga,\
> the one who knows Vajrolī is a yogin and a vessel of accomplishment."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.83

Vajrolī is declared as knowledge rather than prohibition. Its authority arises from understanding that has reorganized impulse at its root.

## The Conditions That Permit Mastery

The practice presupposes conditions already tuned toward steadiness. Nourishment must calm rather than excite, and relationship must cohere with regulation rather than scatter it.

> **tatra vastu-dvayaṃ vakṣye durlabhaṃ yasya kasyacit**\
> **kṣīraṃ caikaṃ dvitīyaṃ tu nārī ca vaśa-vartinī**
>
> "Here I will state two things, difficult for anyone to obtain:\
> first, milk; second, a woman who is under one's governance."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.84

These are prerequisites in the text's own register: cooling, stabilizing nourishment, and a sexual relation that does not scatter the practice. Without them, Vajrolī remains mechanical or incomplete.

## Reversal of the Downward Current

Here the body learns obedience to awareness. What once moved downward without consent is trained to respond.

> **mehanena śanaiḥ samyag ūrdhvākuñcanam abhyaset**\
> **puruṣo'py athavā nārī vajrolī-siddhim āpnuyāt**
>
> "By the penis, one should gradually and correctly practice upward contraction;\
> whether man or woman, one may attain accomplishment in Vajrolī."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.85

Loss is reversed.

> **nārī-bhage patad-binduṃ abhyāsenordhvam āharet**\
> **calitaṃ ca nijaṃ bindum ūrdhvam ākṛṣya rakṣayet**
>
> "Through practice one draws upward the bindu that has fallen in the woman's yoni,\
> and one's own bindu, once moved, is drawn upward and preserved."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.87

## Direction as Destiny

Life and death are described as directions.

> **evaṃ saṃrakṣayed binduṃ jayati yogavit**\
> **maraṇaṃ bindu-pātena jīvanaṃ bindu-dhāraṇāt**
>
> "Thus the knower of yoga preserves bindu and prevails:\
> death comes from the fall of bindu; life comes from its retention."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.88

Here essence translates *bindu*, the concentrated vital seed-drop the practice conserves. Stability announces itself as clarity, as a body no longer leaking vitality into time.

> **sugandho yogino dehe jāyate bindu-dhāraṇāt**\
> **yāvad binduḥ sthiro dehe tāvat kāla-bhayaṃ kutaḥ**
>
> "Fragrance arises in the yogin's body from retaining bindu;\
> while bindu is steady in the body, from where could fear of time arise?"\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.89

Physiology and awareness are bound into a single discipline.

> **cittāyattaṃ nṝṇāṃ śukraṃ śukrāyattaṃ ca jīvitam**\
> **tasmāc chukraṃ manaś caiva rakṣaṇīyaṃ prayatnataḥ**
>
> "For human beings, semen depends on the mind, and life depends on semen;\
> therefore semen and mind alike are to be guarded with care."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.90

## One Principle, Three Expressions

Vajrolī unfolds as a triad without division of essence.

> **sahajolīś cāmarolir vajrolyā bheda ekataḥ**
>
> "Sahajolī and Amarolī are differentiated from Vajrolī while remaining one in principle."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.92, interpretive rendering

Vajrolī governs. Sahajolī eases that governance into nature. Amarolī (the urine-recycling practice, *amara* meaning the deathless) completes the circuit by returning what is refined so nothing essential is wasted.

## Sahajolī: When Effort Falls Away

At maturity, Vajrolī ceases to feel like a technique. Its movement becomes continuous, and the text gives that continuity a name.

> **sahajolir iyaṃ proktā śraddheyā yogibhiḥ sadā**\
> **ayaṃ śubha-karo yogo bhoga-yukto'pi muktidaḥ**
>
> "This is called Sahajolī and should always be trusted by yogins;\
> this auspicious yoga grants liberation even when joined with enjoyment."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.94

Sahajolī (the spontaneous, effortless form of the same retention) names the condition in which enjoyment no longer causes dispersal because regulation has become innate.

## Amarolī: The Returned Current

The *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* gives Amarolī as a concrete practice, not as an allegory. Its rule is the same as the *Ḍāmara Tantra* rule: the middle flow is kept, while the first and last portions are left.

> **amarīṃ yaḥ pibed nityaṃ nasyaṃ kurvan dine dine**\
> **vajrolīm abhyaset samyak sāmarolīti kathyate**
>
> "One who regularly drinks amarī, applies it nasally day by day,\
> and correctly practices Vajrolī is said to be practicing Amarolī."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.96–97

## Unification and Completion

What was first conserved is reintegrated. What was divided is made one.

> **sa bindus tad rajaś caiva ekībhūya svadehagau**\
> **vajroly-abhyāsa-yogena sarva-siddhiṃ prayacchataḥ**
>
> "That bindu and that rajas, becoming one within one's own body,\
> bestow every accomplishment through the yoga of Vajrolī practice."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.101

Here *rajas* is the female generative current that *bindu* is joined with. The culmination is the transfiguration of life.

> **deha-siddhiṃ ca labhate vajroly-abhyāsa-yogataḥ**\
> **ayaṃ puṇya-karo yogo bhoge bhukte'pi muktidaḥ**
>
> "Through the yoga of Vajrolī practice one attains bodily perfection;\
> this merit-making yoga grants liberation even amid enjoyment."\
> — *Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā* 3.103

Where effort dissolves into nature, the body no longer spends itself downward but circulates upward.
